# Login

To access the official ISC Typst Editor, you need a **SWITCH-EDU ID** account. Any member of the ISC team can access the editor.

## Step by step

1. When you arrive on the site, you will be redirected to the **ISC SSO** login page.
2. Enter your SWITCH-EDU ID credentials.
3. Once authenticated, you will be redirected to your **dashboard**.

## Logout

To log out, click the logout button in the top-right corner of the dashboard. This will:

- Destroy your current session
- Log you out from the SSO as well (with your confirmation)
