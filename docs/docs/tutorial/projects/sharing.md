# Collaborating on a project

The **TISC Editor** has a collaboration system that lets you work with your team or your colleagues on a project.

## Management

### Sharing a project

The first step is to share your project with a colleague.

1. On the dashboard, click on the **...** menu.
2. When the modal appears, enter the email of the person you want to share the project with.

:::tip[Email to use]

You must use the `hevs` email.

:::

3. Choose if you want the person to edit the project. By default the person can only view the project.

4. The person will see the project in their dashboard, and you will see a tag next to it indicating that the project is shared.

### Removing a user from the project

When you click on **Share** on the dashboard, you also have the list of people who have access to the project. You can remove them here by clicking on the trash icon.

### Leaving the project

If you were invited to the project, you can leave it without any problem.

If you are the owner, you need to transfer ownership to someone else. You can either:

- Select a new owner when you leave the project, or
- Stay in the project and transfer ownership from the share modal.

## Seeing collaborators in the editor

When you are in the editor, you can see the people collaborating with you by hovering over the component at the top middle of the screen. The list of users will appear.

## How actions impact others

When someone creates or deletes a file, other users see a notification at the top right of the screen.

If a user sets another file as the main file to compile, this change applies to everyone and changes the rendered output for all collaborators.
